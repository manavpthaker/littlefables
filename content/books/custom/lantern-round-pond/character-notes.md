# Character notes — The Lantern of Round Pond

Paste these blocks **verbatim** into every prompt. Do not paraphrase, do not
"improve" the wording between pages — small wording drift is what makes a face
change between page three and page six.

## Rosa (main character)

```text
Rosa, a five-year-old girl with dark curly hair loose to her shoulders, warm
brown skin, a serious watchful face, wearing a forest-green cardigan over a
cream dress and scuffed brown boots.
```

## Grandma June (appears pages 1 and 8)

```text
Grandma June, an older woman with silver hair pinned up, warm brown skin,
half-moon reading glasses, an oxblood shawl over her shoulders.
```

## Recurring objects

The lantern is the book's anchor — it must look identical every time it appears.

```text
The lantern: a small square brass lantern with four glass panes and a ring
handle, mounted on a weathered wooden post at the pond's edge.
```

## Continuity

The whole story is one continuous evening. Light moves in one direction and
never goes back:

| Page | Time | Light |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Late afternoon | Full warm daylight |
| 2 | Early evening | Sun low, long shadows |
| 3 | Early evening | Same as page 2 |
| 4 | Sunset | Honey, then tea-coloured |
| 5 | Dusk | Colour draining, air going blue |
| 6 | The moment | Lantern lit, everything else dim |
| 7 | Just after | Lantern warm, sky deep blue |
| 8 | Blue dark | Lantern behind, cottage light ahead |

Pages 2 and 3 are the same moment — keep their lighting identical.
