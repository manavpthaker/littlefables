Bramble is a small cozy brown bear cub with warm chestnut fur, a honey-toned watercolor wash, lighter cream muzzle and belly, soft rounded paws, tiny round ears, small black nose, gentle small smile, expressive dark eyes, shy but brave posture.

Watercolor direction: wet-on-wet washes, soft pigment blooms, translucent layered color, loose botanical edges, airy paper-cream negative space, gentle handmade paper texture, cozy bedtime palette of paper cream, terracotta, marigold, honey, sage green, and teal. Keep Bramble's approved character design, but paint scenes with softer edges and less rendered fur detail.

Mose support character: Mose is an enormous gentle grizzly bear with deep umber-brown fur, rounded ears, broad dinner-plate paws, soft kind eyes, a calm warm smile, and a protective posture; he towers over Bramble but never looks threatening.
