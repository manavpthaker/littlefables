# Character Notes: When Can We Talk?

Use these blocks verbatim in every generated scene where the character appears. Azad and Lokjit are real human children. Use the supplied private reference photos in `reference images/` for likeness only; do not copy raw family photos into any new public repo location.

## Azi / Azad

Azi/Azad: preschool-age human boy with Colombian and Indian family heritage, warm medium-brown skin, soft round cheeks, large dark expressive eyes, and dense tousled black curls forming a rounded halo with ringlets across his forehead and around his ears. Small red thread bracelet. Curious, thoughtful, playful; often leaning close, sitting on the floor, barefoot, with a guitar, keyboard, puzzle, or family nearby. Modern everyday child clothes only; never plush, animal-like, furry, monkey-like, muzzle-faced, or dressed in ethnic/traditional clothing unless the page text explicitly calls for it.

## Lokijit / Loki

Lokijit/Loki: young human boy close to Azi's age with smooth warm light-to-medium skin, straight dark short hair with a soft side sweep or wispy fringe, rounded forehead, soft cheeks, dark eyes, and an easy gentle smile. He is observant, affectionate, and often shoulder-to-shoulder with Azi, sometimes with an arm around him or a folded paper crane nearby. Never plush or animal-like; do not give him Azi's curly hair.

## Book-Specific Style Layer

Layer the standard Little Fables watercolor anchor with Nianhua (年画) / New Year picture influence: crisp hand-carved woodblock linework, flat decorative color fields, paper-cream ground, terracotta framing, marigold and honey accents, sage and teal cloud-and-wave motifs, tiny floral ornaments, moon and sun medallions, subtle pigment bleed, and light block misregistration.

Keep the Nianhua treatment story-specific. Do not add generic Spring Festival imagery, readable Chinese text, couplets, banners, dragons, fireworks, logos, or costume-like cultural wardrobe. Keep modern everyday clothes.

Interpret the requested "photorealistic art dream state" as photo-grounded likeness and realistic human proportions within a luminous dreamlike painting. Do not make the output look like an unpainted camera photo.
