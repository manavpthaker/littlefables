# Azad ZOOMS Page Art Prompts

Nine images: one cover at 1:1, eight pages at 4:3. Every prompt below is
complete and should be pasted whole.

Approved style source: `previews/APPROVED-prompt.txt`, copied from the approved
Dog Man register in `character-notes.md`.

Use `reference/child-photo.jpg` only as a private likeness reference during
generation. Azad is the boy on the left in the reference photo. Do not copy the
second child into any image. Do not invent parents, coaches, siblings, crowds,
or any other human faces. If support is needed, use only off-frame hands,
objects, animals, nature, or the existing speaking non-human co-stars.

Save as `cover.png` and `pages/01.png` through `pages/08.png` in this folder.

## Cover

`cover.png` (1:1):

```text
Scene description: square book cover image with no readable title text, sunset garden path, warm orange light, cream paper background, Azad riding the little red moto as the single clear focal point, soccer ball and hockey puck cheering from the path edge as tiny non-human co-stars, friendly moon just beginning to rise in the sky, one red helmet accent and road-brown dust puffs, mood of zooming pride and focus.
Azad: preschool boy, age 3–4. Dense, dark, beautifully curly shoulder-length hair. Warm olive skin. Big expressive dark eyes with long lashes. Round soft cheeks. Red thread bracelet on right wrist (keep across scenes). Wears a navy soccer kit + cleats on the field, a blue-and-white hockey jersey with helmet-and-cage on the ice, a red helmet + navy tee + everyday shorts on the moto, and cozy PJs at bedtime.
The little red moto (page 6) — small kid-scale moto (Strider-style push-moto or small electric kid moto). Bright red. Round headlight-face. Rumbles.
The soccer ball (pages 2–3) — classic black-and-white pentagon ball. Slightly expressive face is fine in the Dog Man register (googly eyes / a mouth), but keep it warm not gross. Cheers loudly.
The puck (pages 4–5) — small black hockey puck. Same treatment — a little face is on-register for Dog Man's style; keep it kid-friendly.
The moon (pages 7–8) — soft-faced friendly moon through the bedroom window. Big, warm, sleepy.
Bold, kid-friendly comic-book illustration inspired by Dav Pilkey's Dog Man — thick confident black outlines, punchy flat color with light halftone or hand-drawn texture, dramatic action poses, playful sense of motion (speed lines, dust puffs, wobbly ground when things hit). Sound-effect words (BOOT!, WHOOSH!, GOAL!, WHISH!, CLACK!, VROOM!) rendered as visual elements integrated into the scene — big, bold, hand-lettered feel, colored to pop against the background — because they carry as much of the page as the illustration. Punchy warm palette: grass green, ice blue, sunset orange, road brown, moto red, bedroom moon-silver. One clear focal moment per spread with generous white/cream space; keep compositions readable at book-size.

Bedtime pages (7–8) soften the same style — same bold outlines and hand-drawn energy, but calmer poses, cooler moon-lit blues and silvers, smaller/quieter sound-effect words, no speed lines. Same book, gentler beat.

No text UI, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealism, no invented human faces beyond Azad, no gore or gross-out (Dog Man's gentler register, not the Cat Kid / potty humor side). Cream paper background is fine; a light halftone / kid-drawn wobble is welcome.
```
