# Art prompts — The Lantern of Round Pond

Nine images: one cover at 1:1, eight pages at 4:3. Every prompt below is
complete — paste it whole, no assembly needed.

**Do them in order.** Generate the cover first, then attach it as a style
reference (`--sref` / `--cref`, Flux Redux, Gemini image-to-image) on every page
prompt. That single step matters more than anything else here.

**Reject and regenerate** any output with visible text, a different Rosa, a
palette that drifts pastel or neon, or a lantern that changes shape.

Save as `cover.png` and `pages/01.png` … `pages/08.png` in this folder.

---

## Cover — 1:1

```text
hand-painted storybook illustration in layered gouache and watercolor, visible
brushwork, the richness of a mid-century illustrated classic, aged palette (aged
ivory #EDE3CE, walnut ink #2A1D12, oxblood #7D2E2B, tarnished brass #A67C3A,
pre-war forest green #2E4B3B, burnished gilt #B89154, deep navy #233450), one
clear focal moment, natural composition, gentle depth, no text, no logos, no
watermarks, no photorealism, no harsh contrast, no screens or modern tech

Book cover for "The Lantern of Round Pond". Rosa, a five-year-old girl with dark
curly hair loose to her shoulders, warm brown skin, a serious watchful face,
wearing a forest-green cardigan over a cream dress and scuffed brown boots. She
sits alone on the grassy bank of a small round pond, knees drawn up, looking
steadily at a small square brass lantern with four glass panes and a ring
handle, mounted on a weathered wooden post at the water's edge. The lantern is
lit and glows warm gold. Deep blue evening sky above, the last band of honey
light at the horizon. Her reflection and the lantern's sit together on still
water. Composition centers Rosa with the lantern to her right and room above for
breath.
```

## Page 06 — 4:3

*And then, without any fuss at all, the lantern lit.*

The book's turning point. Give it the most attention.

```text
[COVER STYLE ANCHOR]

Scene illustration. The moment the lantern lights. The small square brass
lantern with four glass panes and a ring handle, on its weathered wooden post,
suddenly glowing warm gold — the only bright thing in the frame. Everything else
has gone to deep blue dusk. No hand, no match, no figure near it. The water
below is perfectly still, no ripple at all. Rosa, a five-year-old girl with dark
curly hair loose to her shoulders, warm brown skin, wearing a forest-green
cardigan over a cream dress, sits close by with her face lit gold from one side,
eyes wide, entirely still.

Composition: medium, the lantern and Rosa balanced, the lantern the clear focal
point. Mood: wonder — big quiet, small character, soft awe. No text, no logos,
no watermarks.
```
