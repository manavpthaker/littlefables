# Full-book art prompt bundle — Averill · Take It With You, Averill

Use with `story.json`, `character-notes.md`, `reference/child-photo.jpg` as a private likeness reference if available, and `previews/APPROVED-cover.png` as the approved cover/style reference.

## Non-negotiable rules

- **Cast:** Just Averill — no other humans in the art. No invented parents, coaches, siblings, friends, crowds, adult hands, arms, shadows, or faces.
- **IP boundary:** no Disney Cars, Disney Planes, Lion King, WWE, Lightning McQueen, Simba, Dusty, Skye, Chick Hicks, branded race cars, branded wrestlers, logos, sponsor decals, or recognizable IP.
- **No readable text:** no letters, numbers, words, captions, speech balloons, UI, book title, signatures, watermarks, shirt writing, screen menus, toy-bin labels, or car door numbers.
- **Consistency:** keep the character block and approved style anchor identical across every page. Do not rewrite them.
- **Format:** save `cover.png` from the approved cover exactly. Save interiors as `pages/01.png` through `pages/08.png`, landscape 4:3 Little Fables interior pages.
- **Consistency check every 3 pages:** hair silhouette, bare feet, gray tee pictogram, navy shorts, Zoom stripes/no number, braided rug palette, toy bin/dinosaur, and left-window light direction.
