## The story

**Take It With You, Averill**

_Page 1._ Averill has a screen. On the screen there is a race. VROOM! VROOM! A big red car goes fast. A monster truck ROARS. Averill roars too. "AAAAAROOOOO!" It is the best race ever.

_Page 2._ "Time to be done, buddy." Averill's smile goes small. His fists go tight. His whole body says NO.

_Page 3._ But then — on the rug — someone waits. It is Zoom. Zoom is a small red race car who lives on the rug. Zoom is not on the screen. Zoom is right here.

_Page 4._ "Averill," says Zoom, in a Zoom-shaped voice. "The race is not over. The next lap is out here." Averill blinks. Out here?

_Page 5._ "Try it," says Zoom. "Say the name. Do the thing. Pick where we go." Averill takes a big breath. He knows the name. "ZOOM!" he shouts. His voice is HUGE.

_Page 6._ Now the thing. Averill puts his arms out wide like a monster truck. He STOMPS one foot. STOMP! He ROARS. "AAAAAROOOOO!" The room shakes just a little.

_Page 7._ Now the where. Averill looks around. "KITCHEN," he says. "We go to the KITCHEN." And Zoom in his hand and the roar in his chest and the STOMPS in his feet — they all come with him. VROOM VROOM to the kitchen!

_Page 8._ Later, the world gets a little softer. The kitchen becomes the couch. The couch becomes the bath. The bath becomes the bed. And with every new place, Averill takes Zoom. And the roar. And the wild. All the way down to the pillow, where a small red race car parks in a small warm hand. "Race track will still be there tomorrow," whispers Zoom. And Averill — wild, funny, giant Averill — closes his eyes with a very small VROOM.
