# Preview cover prompts - Averill - take-it-with-you

Generated from `art-prompts-preview.md` in preview mode.

No-readable-text note: `character-notes.md` mentions sound-effect words as part of the desired energy, but `$fable-art-custom` universal safety forbids visible/readable text. These preview prompts use dust puffs, motion streaks, roar shapes, and stomp marks instead of readable lettering.

## Character block

Averill: preschool boy, age 3–4. Fair skin, apple cheeks, straight blonde hair cropped chin-length with a natural side-swept front. Wide open eyes, huge cheeky smile with a hint of missing baby teeth in the corners. Sturdy toddler build. Wears an everyday gray tee with a small graphic across the chest (mountains, a friend, something warm) and comfy shorts or sweats in most scenes. Bath scene: hooded towel with animal ears. Bedtime: cozy PJs (red rocket / race-car print if in doubt).

## Shared scene

Create a vertical children's picture-book cover illustration with no title text. Scene must show just Averill on a warm braided living-room rug, arms stretched wide like a monster truck, one foot stomping, mouth open in a playful roar, with Zoom the toy race car beside him on the rug. Zoom is a small cherry-red toy race car with two white racing stripes, large round friendly headlight-eyes, and a small grille-mouth. No other humans, no adult hands, no faces besides Averill.

Cozy family living room, late-afternoon golden light through one window, warm braided rug in oranges, reds, and cream, toy bin softly visible near the edge, cream paper background texture. No tablet, TV, UI, logos, branded characters, framed readable details, or readable lettering anywhere.

Stable front-cover composition, Averill centered and full body, Zoom in lower foreground angled toward him, clear focal point, generous safe margins, no text area needed. High-energy but bedtime-safe, funny and wild without real peril.

Critical no-text rule: There must be zero letters, numbers, words, captions, UI, logos, signatures, watermarks, or letter-like marks anywhere in the image. Averill's gray shirt may have only a simple mountain/sun/tree icon made of shapes, no writing. Zoom must not have a door number or any markings except two plain white stripes.

Illustrated only, not photorealistic. No Disney Cars, Planes, Lion King, WWE, Lightning McQueen, Simba, Dusty, branded faces, logos, watermarks, UI, or readable words. No other child from the reference photo. Avoid harsh contrast.
