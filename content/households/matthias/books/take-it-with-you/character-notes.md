# Take It With You, Averill — Character Notes

## Cast rule (READ FIRST)

**The only real human in this book is Averill.** The buyer answered
"companions: Just Averill" on the intake — no parents, siblings,
grandparents, coaches, or friends appear in the art. Do not invent any.
Any adult voice in the text (the "Time to be done, buddy" line on page
2) is heard from off-frame — no face, no distinguishing features. Every
other speaking character in the book is a *toy race car on the rug*
named Zoom.

**IP boundary — non-negotiable.** Averill loves Disney's *Cars*,
Disney's *Planes*, *Lion King*, monster trucks, and wrestling. **Do
NOT draw Lightning McQueen, Simba, Chick Hicks, Skye, Dusty, any WWE
wrestler, or any other branded character.** We are selling this book.
Draw archetypes that *echo* those loves — a generic red race car with
racing stripes (Zoom), a chunky black-and-yellow monster truck
silhouette, a wrestler-style muscle-arms pose from Averill himself,
big cartoon dust puffs, roar lines, motion streaks. The visual
*register* is Cars / monster trucks / wrestling; the *characters* are
Averill and Zoom only.

The reference photo at `reference/child-photo.jpg` is a private
likeness anchor. It shows two children — **Averill is the boy on the
LEFT**, fair skin, straight blonde hair, big open smile. The other
child on the right (dark curly hair, plaid shirt) is not part of this
book and must not appear.

## Character

Averill: preschool boy, age 3–4. Fair skin, apple cheeks, straight blonde hair cropped chin-length with a natural side-swept front. Wide open eyes, huge cheeky smile with a hint of missing baby teeth in the corners. Sturdy toddler build. Wears an everyday gray tee with a small graphic across the chest (mountains, a friend, something warm) and comfy shorts or sweats in most scenes. Bath scene: hooded towel with animal ears. Bedtime: cozy PJs (red rocket / race-car print if in doubt).

## Speaking co-star

**Zoom** (pages 3–8): a small red toy race car who lives on Averill's rug when the screen is off. Chunky friendly proportions like an early-reader illustration, not a photorealistic model car. Cherry red body with two plain white racing stripes down the hood; large round expressive headlight-eyes; a small friendly grille-mouth that shifts subtly across pages (encouraging, curious, sleepy). No door number, sponsor decals, letters, or readable markings. Palm-sized in Averill's hand on the last spread. Zoom is *Averill's toy*, not a magical car — no glow, no floating, no sparkles. Zoom's warmth comes from posture, eyes, and being *right there*, not from special effects.

## Non-speaking props by scene

- **Screen scene (page 1):** a modern tablet or TV with a paused-mid-action shot of generic race cars kicking dust on a track. NOT Radiator Springs, NOT Piston Cup, no branded logos. If a monster truck appears on the screen, make it a generic beefy 4×4 in orange or blue with big treads and flame decals. Averill on the rug, mouth open mid-roar, arms up.
- **Rug scene (pages 3–7):** warm braided rug in oranges, reds, cream. Toy bin nearby. A stuffed animal or two around the edges (fine to include — they don't speak). Late-afternoon light coming through a window at low angle.
- **Kitchen / couch (transitional, page 7 or coda):** everyday family kitchen, warm tones, one plate of banana-and-cracker snack on the counter if visible. Couch is soft, worn, welcoming.
- **Bath (coda):** simple family bathroom. Warm tub, one rubber duck, gentle steam. Averill smiling. Zoom perched safely on the edge of the tub, dry.
- **Bed (coda final):** twin bed with cozy duvet in warm reds/creams. Nightstand with a small lamp glowing warm. Window with a soft dusk-blue sky. Zoom parked in Averill's palm on the pillow. Eyes closing.

## Style anchor — APPROVED (Style B, gouache-and-crayon)

Matte gouache-and-crayon children's picture-book illustration with chunky rounded shapes, simple expressive faces, friendly toy-truck warmth, and bouncy early-reader action, bold dark outlines softened by visible crayon texture, kinetic stomp pose with non-letter roar shapes, dust puffs, and curved motion marks, bright but cozy palette of cherry red, marigold, cream, kraft brown, soft peach, and dusty teal, cream paper texture, playful age 3-4 mood, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealism, no harsh contrast, no modern tech.

Across the arc: high-energy compositions and full sunlight on the rug in Act 1 (pages 1–6), sunset warmth on the transition (page 7), and dusk-blue quiet in the coda (page 8) — same character, same rendering style, softer lighting and quieter compositions as the arc lands. Cream paper background throughout.

## No readable text — anywhere

The `fable-art-custom` skill forbids visible text in generated images. This overrides any sound-effect-lettering idea you may see referenced elsewhere. **Do NOT render VROOM / ROAR / STOMP / AAAAROOOOO as visible letters.** Instead:

- Big **dust puffs** around the stomp foot
- **Curved motion streaks** trailing the STOMP and the ROAR
- **Radiating roar-shapes** — abstract sunburst-like marks around Averill's open mouth (no letter forms)
- **Speed lines** behind Zoom in the VROOM moments

The narrator carries the sound effects; the art carries the *physics* of the sound.

Also no readable text elsewhere: no writing on the tee (mountain-sun-tree icon made of *shapes* only), no letters on the toy bin, no words on any tablet/TV screen (paused race image only), no signage. Averill's shirt-graphic must be a small pictogram — mountain + sun + tree silhouette in cream/teal/marigold — not the reference photo's "FRIEND" text.

## Consistency lock-ins (must stay identical across all 8 pages)

These were established on the approved cover (v1-cover-B.png). Do not change them page-to-page.

- **Zoom's markings:** two plain white racing stripes down the hood. **No door number.** No sponsor decals. Small friendly grille-mouth (subtle expression shifts across pages are fine — the mouth itself doesn't change shape/size).
- **Averill's tee:** gray with a small stylized mountain-sun-tree pictogram, no writing.
- **Averill's shorts:** navy-blue soft shorts with a drawstring bow at the waist.
- **Living-room rug:** warm braided oval in cherry red, marigold, cream, soft peach — visible in every indoor page that shows the floor.
- **The toy bin near the rug:** contains a small green stuffed dinosaur and one or two other plush shapes. Same dinosaur, same position in the bin, on every page that shows the bin.
- **Averill's feet:** bare in every indoor scene (until the bath/PJ pages).
- **Light source:** one big window on the left side of the living-room scenes — direction of light stays consistent.

## Must NOT contain

Any branded IP faces (Cars, Planes, WWE, Lion King, Disney), photorealism, text of any kind (see above), UI on any tablet or TV, invented human faces beyond Averill, harsh contrast, gore, potty humor. Adult presence permitted only as an off-frame voice on page 2 — no arm, no shadow, no hand, no legs, nothing.

## Reference notes

- **The interests to echo, not name:** Cars → generic race cars on a track. Planes → skip on-page unless the room shows a plane poster. Lion King → the big AAAAROOOOO in Averill's voice, no lion in the art. Monster trucks → generic 4×4s on the paused screen. Wrestling → Averill's own arms-out-STOMP pose is the wrestler beat.
- **The tool the story teaches is named THREE times:** "Say the name. Do the thing. Pick where we go." (Zoom on page 5 as a whole sentence; Averill's execution on pages 5–7 as three consecutive beats.) The named tool is *Take It With You* — that's the book title and the parent-guide.md anchor.
- **Bedtime-safe:** the arc lands on a whispered goodnight, a very small VROOM, and Zoom in Averill's palm. No cliffhanger, no punchline. This is a book that gets read at 7pm, so it must be safe to close and turn off the light.
