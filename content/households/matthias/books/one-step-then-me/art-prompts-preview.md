## Status and source

Preview mode. No buyer style inspirations or `typeform.json` were present in
the book folder, so these three directions are inferred from the story's
emotional shape: late-summer exploration, two near-peer young children, and the
contrast between a tiny police car and a monumental fire engine.

Private reference photos are available locally and confirmed gitignored.
These are prompts for the manual, operator-controlled likeness workflow only;
do not send the reference folder to an unattended image API.

## Cast rule — exact

The only real humans in this book are Averill and Azi. Both were supplied by
the family. Do not invent additional human faces. Background figures, when
unavoidable, must be distant and faceless.

## Canonical character blocks — exact

**Averill:** Three-and-a-half-year-old preschool boy with fair pink-cheeked
skin, pale blue-grey eyes, ash-blond bob and fringe, longer limbs, lean build,
grey T-shirt, dark trousers, black sneakers.

**Azi:** Four-and-a-half-year-old preschool boy with warm medium-brown skin,
dark eyes, dense black curl halo, longer limbs, coordinated build, beige
gingham shirt, brown corduroys, black sneakers.

**Police car:** Small scuffed die-cast black-and-white police cruiser with
chunky black wheels, white door panels, and a low light bar.

## Locked cover scene — identical in A, B, and C

Square book-cover illustration with no title or lettering. From a low,
child-height camera directly behind them, Averill stands on the left and Azi
on the right, their hands joined between them as they face into the wide-open
mouth of a small-town fire-station bay. Averill's free outer fist holds the
little police car. The boys are near-peers, Azi only slightly taller. Their
two small backs occupy the lower third. Ahead, a real lime-yellow-over-white
fire engine rises from cool bay shadow and runs beyond the top edge of the
frame; chrome catches warm daylight, and one yellow exhaust hose curves high
overhead. The engine is monumental, never cute or toy-sized. Warm light holds
the children at the threshold while the bay ahead is dim and safe. The single
focal relationship is two small joined hands against enormous scale. Mood:
awe, hesitation, and company. No firefighters, parents, crowds, or other
human faces. Do not depict readable station signs, vehicle numbers, letters,
logos, or title text.
