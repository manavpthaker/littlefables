# One Step, Then Me — page art prompts

Style anchor and character blocks live in `character-notes.md`. Use those
descriptions **verbatim** in every prompt; do not paraphrase Averill's or
Azi's appearance per-page.

Two rules carried in from `docs/commerce/fulfillment-playbook.md` step 22
(they came out of the art bake-off and are generator-agnostic, so they bind
the manual ChatGPT path too):

1. Restate the full character description in every single page prompt.
   Never rely on "the same boy as before".
2. Describe the character's position and action in the scene *before*
   describing the background, so the figure anchors the composition.

## Cover

`cover.png` (1:1): Book cover art for "One Step, Then Me", no title text.
Averill and Azi stand together small and centred, seen from behind, hands
joined, facing into the wide-open mouth of a fire-station bay. Averill on
the left in his grey woodland-graphic t-shirt, dark trousers and black
sneakers, ash-blond bob, the little black-and-white police car in his far
fist. Azi on the right in the beige gingham shirt and brown corduroys, dark
curls haloed. Ahead of them the lime-yellow engine looms out of soft shadow,
cropped by the frame so its top is lost. Warm daylight behind the children,
cool cave-dark ahead. Composition: two small figures low in the frame,
enormous doorway arch above them, generous paper-cream breathing room at the
top. Mood: awe, smallness, and company.

## Page scenes

`pages/01.png` (4:3): Averill sits in a car seat holding his little die-cast
police car in one closed fist, looking down at it with quiet attention.
Averill is a small human boy about two and a half, fair skin, pink cheeks,
fine ash-blond chin-length bob with full fringe, pale blue-grey eyes, grey
woodland-graphic t-shirt. The small black-and-white police car is the clear
focal object, warm light falling across his hands. Behind him, softly out of
focus, a car window with late-summer green countryside sliding past.
Composition: close, low, child eye-level, the small fist and small car dead
centre. Mood: private, contented, portable.

`pages/02.png` (4:3): Averill stands small in a big open family room looking
up and around at a wall of doors, the police car in his fist; Azi is coming
toward him from one of the doorways. Averill in his grey woodland-graphic
t-shirt and dark trousers, ash-blond bob; Azi about three and a half, roughly
a year older and only a little taller, warm medium-brown skin, dense tousled black curls, beige-and-white gingham
shirt and brown corduroys. The room is generous and slightly overwhelming:
pale walls, blond hardwood, dark leather sofa, a big patterned grey-and-cream
rug, several glass-paned interior doors, tall windows with green trees.
Composition: wide, ceiling high in frame, Averill small in the lower third
with doors receding around him. Mood: a house too big to hold at once — and
then a friend in it.

`pages/03.png` (4:3): Averill and Azi crouch together at the edge of a
shallow woodland creek, both looking down into moving water. Averill on the
left, grey t-shirt, ash-blond bob, police car still in one fist; Azi beside
him in gingham shirt and corduroys, dark curls, one hand braced on a rock.
Clear shallow water runs over smooth grey-brown stones; ferns and heavy
late-summer green woods crowd both banks; dappled sunlight breaks through
the canopy onto the surface. Composition: medium-close, low to the water,
the creek running diagonally across the frame. Mood: cool, hushed, absorbing
— the water is the loudest thing here.

`pages/04.png` (4:3): Azi has just hopped onto a flat stepping stone
mid-creek and is turning back over his shoulder toward Averill, one hand
reaching behind him. Azi in his beige gingham shirt, brown corduroys, dark
curls, body mid-balance and face open and waiting — not urging. Averill
still on the bank, fist closed around the police car, one foot lifting. A
line of flat stones crosses the shallow water between them. Composition:
both children in profile-to-three-quarter across the creek, the gap of water
between them as the true subject. Mood: an offer, playful and small-stakes —
this is the rehearsal of the whole book.

`pages/05.png` (4:3): Averill and Azi stand at a wooden post-and-rail fence,
both small against it, looking out at two horses grazing in a mown green
field. Averill on the left with his fist still closed around the police car,
Azi beside him with one hand on the rail. The horses — one chestnut bay, one
pale — are at middle distance with heads down, entirely unbothered. Heavy
late-summer woods behind, a long cream barn at the right edge, bright
morning sun. Composition: children small in the lower left third, fence
leading the eye across to the horses, wide sky. Mood: enormous and gentle;
bigness that does not mind you.

`pages/06.png` (4:3): Averill and Azi seen small from behind at the open
mouth of a fire-station bay, the enormous roll-up door standing wide with
its white painted jamb rising past the top of the frame. Averill on the
left, grey t-shirt, ash-blond bob, police car in his fist; Azi on the right,
gingham shirt, dark curls. Inside, dim and cave-cool, a lime-yellow-over-
white fire engine sits nose-out with chrome trim and red-and-white chevron
striping, bright yellow coiled exhaust hoses hanging in long curves from the
ceiling. The engine must read as overwhelmingly larger than any vehicle a
toddler has a name for — this is the "bigger than a monster truck" page.
Composition: low camera at child height, the doorway a huge dark rectangle,
the two children tiny at its base. Mood: something huge and sleeping.

`pages/07.png` (4:3): Averill stands stopped on the concrete apron, feet
planted and slightly apart, both shoulders drawn up, staring in through the
bay door with his fist clenched tight around the little police car. Averill
in his grey woodland-graphic t-shirt, dark trousers, black sneakers,
ash-blond bob, pale blue-grey eyes wide. Azi stands beside him — not ahead,
beside — turned toward him and settled, clearly in no hurry. A comfortable
band of sunlight holds them both outside the dark doorway. Composition:
close on Averill, low, the clenched fist and planted feet both readable; the
engine's yellow bulk a soft looming mass behind. Mood: feet gone heavy — the
body saying no before the mouth does — and permission to stay put.

`pages/08.png` (4:3): Azi has taken one single step toward the bay and is
turning back over his shoulder to look at Averill, one hand beginning to
reach behind him — deliberately the same pose and gesture as page 4, now on
concrete instead of creek stones. Azi in his beige gingham shirt, brown
corduroys, dark curls, face open and waiting. Averill still standing where
he was, fist closed, considering. A clear gap of maybe two paces between
them on the grey concrete. Composition: mirror page 4 as closely as the
settings allow — same camera height, same left-right placement, same space
between the children. Mood: an offer, not a pull. The echo is the point.

`pages/09.png` (4:3): Averill and Azi from directly behind, hands joined
between them, walking together into the bay — the anchor image of the book.
Averill on the left in the grey t-shirt, ash-blond bob; Azi on the right in
the beige gingham shirt, dark curls. Ahead of them the fire engine rears up
enormous, deliberately cropped so its cab runs out of the top of the frame,
chrome and lime-yellow catching the light, the coiled yellow hose curving
overhead. Composition: low and central, the two small backs and one joined
hand in the lower third, the machine towering and unbounded above. Mood: the
thing getting bigger and bigger, and going anyway.

`pages/10.png` (4:3): Averill stands up inside the engine cab, small in a
dim close space, looking around with his mouth slightly open. A row of red
and clear dome lights is set into the low ceiling just above him. High black
seats far too big for him rise on either side; coiled radio cables, hanging
harnesses, white helmets racked at the side, red equipment bags. Azi is
right there beside him, half a step away, steady. Daylight comes through the
cab windows in one bright band across them both. Composition: interior,
close, low ceiling pressing down, the two children small and central. This
page is the turn-of-the-page reveal — make it feel like arriving. Mood:
inside the big thing — cave-dark, astonishing, safe.

`pages/11.png` (4:3): The little black-and-white police car sits alone on a
bedside table, catching the last warm edge of lamplight. Behind it and
softly out of focus, Averill asleep or nearly asleep under a blanket,
ash-blond hair against the pillow. Nothing else in the frame. Composition:
very close and still, the small police car sharp in the foreground, the
whole rest of the image soft. Mood: quiet, complete, bedtime — the smallest
possible object holding the biggest possible day.
