STYLE ANCHOR — paste verbatim into every art prompt for *The Fort in Coventry*.
Approved 2026-08-15 as the fresh direction for this book. Do not paraphrase.
Second matthias book — a deliberate visual break from the cut-paper collage of
one-step-then-me.

Sendak-style ink and watercolor. Crosshatched pen line drawn on warm cream
stock, transparent watercolor washes laid over the ink, visible paper texture
through the paint, brushwork rough at the edges of shapes, ink pooling in the
crosshatch, small halos of raw cream paper left where highlights would land.

Palette:
  forest green (foliage, fort dark, horse-field distance)
  dusk blue (interior shadow, night sky, water)
  warm cream (paper stock, highlights, morning light)
  deep umber (crosshatch, hair, wood, hooves)
  terracotta (Averill's cheeks, brick, warm side of the palette)
  one saturated fire-engine red — used sparingly across the book and full-
  tilt on page 12 for the sleeping giant

Line weight varies with mood:
  tight and fine for the big house pages (1–3)
  looser and shakier at the mythic-portal pages (7, 9, 11–12)
  densest, warmest crosshatch inside the fort (page 4)

Read-aloud lineage: Where the Wild Things Are, In the Night Kitchen, Outside
Over There. Kids on adventure, real objects made monumental at child-height,
dream logic without menace. The fort is a warm and slightly magical space,
not scary. The mythic pages are big and strange but not threatening. The
sleeping giant on page 12 is awe, not fear.

NEVER:
  no airbrushed shading, no smooth digital gradients
  no glossy 3D clay/felt/plastic look
  no photorealism
  no visible text, readable letters, logos, watermarks
  no UI, no screens, no modern tech unless the story requires it
  no invented humans — Averill and Azi only, no distant faceless figures
  no autumn foliage (the trip was late summer, everything full green)
  no cute or toy-sized fire engine on pages 11–12 — scale is the subject
