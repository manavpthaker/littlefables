# Page art prompts — *The Fort in Coventry*

Fourteen interior spreads plus one cover. Every prompt below stands alone
and is meant to be pasted into the image tool with three companion files
appended verbatim:

1. `previews/APPROVED-prompt.txt` (style anchor)
2. `character-notes.md` § "Canonical character blocks" (identity locks)
3. `character-notes.md` § "Cast rule" (no invented humans)

If you generate with fal / GPT Image 2 per `docs/commerce/fulfillment-
playbook.md` step 22, follow the two prompt-design rules that came out of
the 2026-08-09 bake-off (they are generator-agnostic and are named in the
playbook).

Aspect: 3:2 landscape for interior pages, 1:1 for cover. Text will overlay
in the reader; leave the bottom third quieter than the top.

---

## Cover

Averill and Azi from behind, holding hands, standing at the wide-open door
of a small blanket-and-cushion fort tucked into the corner of a pale-walled
Airbnb living room. Warm daylight coming from the tall window on the left,
green trees visible outside. The fort is dark inside, one narrow slot of
light spilling out of it. Averill is holding his die-cast police car in his
right hand. The whole scene is framed slightly from above — a child-height
camera looking down at the two small explorers about to enter their fort.

Palette: dominant warm cream and dusk blue with terracotta accents; one
tiny spark of fire-engine red on the police car. Sendak-style crosshatched
ink line, transparent watercolor washes, visible cream paper texture. Title
"The Fort in Coventry" is not drawn — reader chrome overlays it.

Do not include: adults, other children, autumn foliage, visible text or
letters, glossy/smooth rendering, photorealism.

---

## Page 1 — "The big house had so many doors."

Wide establishing spread of the Airbnb great-room. Pale walls, blond
hardwood, dark leather sofa on the right, patterned grey-and-cream area
rug. **Twelve doors visible or clearly implied**: glass-paned interior
doors, a hallway leading off with more doors down its length, a screen
door out to the trees. Tall windows on the left, green summer trees
outside. Averill sits cross-legged on the rug near the center, small
against the scale of the room, counting on his fingers, mouth mid-word.
Azi is behind him, watching. Averill's police car is on the rug at his
knee.

Palette: warm cream and dusk blue dominant, deep umber for the sofa and
door frames, forest green in the outside trees. Line weight tight and
fine — this is the "real world" register.

Scale note: the room reads *too big for them*. Ceiling high in the
frame. The boys are small in the composition.

---

## Page 2 — "Behind one door, a tiny nook."

Averill and Azi from the side, in the corner of an unused nook in the big
house — a shallow alcove under a staircase, or the recess between two
built-in bookcases. Just tall enough for a preschooler to stand upright.
One of the many doors from page 1 is visible in the background, ajar,
letting light in from the great-room. The nook itself is dim. Both boys
are looking up into it with the "this could work" expression.

Palette: dusk blue dominant, warm cream from the open door, deep umber for
the woodwork. Line weight tight and fine still — one page from now the
line will loosen.

Composition: the boys occupy the lower half; the nook rises above and
around them, framing them.

---

## Page 3 — "Averill and Azi dragged in a blanket."

Mid-action spread: Averill and Azi hauling a blanket, a wooden chair, and
two big cushions into the nook. Show one of them dragging the chair, the
other with the blanket bunched up over his shoulder like a cape. Azi in
the lead position, Averill following with the police car still clenched in
his non-hauling hand. A cushion abandoned partway on the hardwood between
the great-room and the nook. Their motion is *purposeful* — this is
construction, not play.

Palette: same as page 2. Loosen the line very slightly — a hint that the
world is about to change.

---

## Page 4 — "FORT."

Interior of the finished fort. Warm dark. One narrow shaft of daylight
leaking in through the blanket's slit at the top-left. Airborne dust motes
in the light. The two boys sit cross-legged inside, knee to knee, faces
lit warm from the light and cool from the dusk-blue shadow. Averill's
police car sits on the cushion between them, facing outward.

**This is the densest crosshatch page in the book.** Warm cream ground,
deep-umber crosshatch for the fort interior, terracotta glow on the boys'
cheeks, one clean shaft of raw paper for the light. The word "FORT" is
NOT drawn — reader chrome overlays it.

Composition: symmetric, low camera, both boys facing the reader as if
inviting them in. This is the emotional threshold of the book. Get it
right.

---

## Page 5 — "Averill's little police car crackled."

Close on Averill and Azi inside the fort, leaning forward toward the
police car on the cushion between them. Averill's hand hovers over it.
Azi's ear is cocked toward it. The car has a **tiny warm-glowing light on
the roof** — drawn as a soft watercolor bloom of terracotta-orange, not
as a special-effect line. That is the radio speaking.

Palette: dusk blue and deep umber dominant (still inside the fort), one
warm terracotta glow at the center, small halo of raw cream paper around
the light. Line looser than page 4 — the fort dark is *listening*.

Scale: the car reads slightly larger than a realistic die-cast toy would.
The mythic register is beginning.

---

## Page 6 — "Azi went first, like always."

Split composition — Azi mid-crawl, exiting the fort's *other side* (a
different opening from the one they entered), disappearing into a
wash of forest green that has no business being inside the Airbnb.
Averill is behind him, one foot forward, watching. The fort's interior
frame is still visible around them, but the destination side is
unmistakably *elsewhere*.

Palette: fort dusk-blue on the left, forest green intruding on the right.
This is the *transition* page — the moment the reader realizes what the
fort is. Loosen line further.

Composition: horizontal split. Left half is the interior we know from
pages 4–5. Right half is the new world beginning to bloom.

---

## Page 7 — "Out the fort's other side — into a creek."

Mythic creek at child-height. Averill and Azi mid-stride on a rock in the
middle of a shallow woodland creek. **Reeds are taller than Azi.** Rocks
in the water are the size of preschooler shoes and each other. Dappled
light through overhead leaves — leaf-shadows across the boys' arms and
faces. Both boys have their mouths open, mid-shout — the "SKUNGAMAUG!"
moment. Averill's police car is in his fist.

Palette: forest green dominant, water-silver and wet slate, deep umber
under the reeds. One small terracotta glow at Averill's cheek. Line loose
and shakier — full mythic register now. No identifiable landmark
invented; the creek is *a* creek, not this particular creek. No autumn
foliage.

Do not include: adults, other children, footpath, signage.

---

## Page 8 — "Back through the fort. Out the other side."

Small transition beat. Split composition again, mirrored from page 6:
left half is the fort interior (fleeting glimpse); right half is a new
world beginning to bloom, this time in the warm ochres and dusk greens
of open pasture. The boys are mid-crawl through the fort's other side,
Azi still in the lead, Averill right behind. Under-eye view — the reader
looks up at them from the destination side.

Palette: fort dusk-blue on the left, pasture ochre-green on the right.
Line loose. Small.

---

## Page 9 — "— a horse field."

Post-and-rail wooden fence in the foreground, cropped low. **Two horses
drawn at unnerving preschooler-eye scale** — one chestnut bay coming down
toward the reader with its nose the size of a door swinging shut, one
pale horse in the middle distance grazing. Teeth long as a hand.
Hooves large enough to fill the lower quarter of the page. Averill and
Azi stand at the fence, tiny in the composition, both looking up. Averill
has his police car raised — showing the horses the car? offering it? the
gesture reads as *presenting evidence*.

Palette: pasture ochre-green dominant, deep umber for the fence and the
chestnut horse, dusk-blue in the trees behind. Line loose.

**Not scary — awed.** The horses do not mind the boys at all. Their eyes
are calm, not wide. This is monumental scale, not menace.

Do not include: adults, other children, riders, tack, barn interiors.

---

## Page 10 — "Back in the fort, the police car crackled."

Back inside the fort, same interior as pages 4–5, but the mood has
shifted. Both boys are visibly tired — Azi rubbing an eye, Averill
sitting back on his heels. The police car is between them again, the
warm-glowing light on its roof brighter than on page 5. A crackle
implied.

Palette: dusk blue and deep umber, the terracotta glow slightly stronger
than page 5. Line pulls tight again briefly — we are back in the "safe"
register — but the light on the car is louder.

Composition: low camera. The car is the loudest thing in the frame.

---

## Page 11 — "Averill went first."

**This is the emotional beat of the book. The illustration must carry it.**

Averill in the lead, walking out of the fort's other side onto **grey
concrete**. Wide-wide-place stretching before him. One enormous roll-up
door of the fire station standing wide open on the far side of the
concrete apron — white-painted jamb, catenary curves of coiled yellow
exhaust-extraction hose visible in the shadows inside. Azi is one step
behind, watching Averill with a small expression of *oh — you're doing
it*. Averill has the police car held in front of him at chest height, the
warm-glowing light on its roof pointed forward.

Averill's posture: shoulders forward, chin up, stride confident. He is
not running; he is *approaching on purpose*. This is not the same body
as pages 6, 7, 9 where he was second. **The reversal must read from his
body first, the composition second.**

Palette: concrete grey, dusk blue morning shadow, warm cream of the
door's painted jamb, forest green trees behind the station. One small
terracotta glow at the police car. Line tight (Averill's focus) but the
edges of the door catenary curves loose (the mythic still hovering).

Composition: Averill in the near-mid-ground, off-center forward; Azi
back-left of him; the door of the fire station large in the far frame
but not yet showing what is inside.

Do not include: adults, other emergency crew, other trucks visible from
the apron, signage.

---

## Page 12 — "And inside — something ENORMOUS."

**Interior of the fire bay, child-height camera, low.** The sleeping
giant fills the frame: a fire engine drawn as a *sleeping beast*.
Fire-engine red body, chrome accents, closed headlights, quiet posture,
no crew visible, no other trucks in the bay. Coiled yellow exhaust-
extraction hoses hanging in long catenary curves from the ceiling. Grey
painted floor. **The engine is cropped beyond the frame** — the reader
cannot see all of it. Nose in the mid-ground, wheel in the near
foreground, upper cab exiting the top of the page.

Averill and Azi stand small in the near-foreground corner, dwarfed by
the wheel. They are still. Averill still holds the police car, its warm
light dimmed here — the little sibling in the presence of the elder.

Palette: **this is the fire-engine red page.** Saturated red dominant.
Chrome mirror-bright with cream-paper halos. Grey concrete floor. Dusk-
blue shadow around the beast. Line densest here since page 4 — the
mythic *lives here*.

Scale: monumental. The engine must not read as toy. The child must not
read as safe from awe. Read *In the Night Kitchen*'s milk-bottle spread
for reference on scale.

Do not include: crew, other trucks, other children, adults, hoses
uncoiled, alarm lights on, signage, readable letters or numbers on the
engine.

---

## Page 13 — "Found it," Averill whispered.

Close two-shot: Averill and Azi shoulder to shoulder, faces lit warm-red
from the giant offscreen right. Averill holds the police car up between
them, its warm light going BLINK — draw as a slightly brighter bloom
than any previous page, still watercolor-bloom not special-effect.
Averill's expression is not triumph — it is *knowing*. Azi's expression
is agreement. Their eyes are half-lidded, quiet, decided.

Palette: warm cream and terracotta on the boys' faces; deep umber
crosshatch behind them; the reflected red of the giant on the right
side of both faces. Line tight — this is a decision moment.

Composition: close crop. The reader is inside the moment with them.

Do not include: the giant visible in-frame (it is offscreen — the light
on the boys' faces does the work).

---

## Page 14 — "Back through the fort. Back through the small door."

Bedside coda. Night interior of a bedroom in the Airbnb — pale walls,
tall window with dark trees outside, a fabric-shaded bedside lamp on
low. Averill in bed under a light patterned quilt, on his side, eyes
closed but not fully asleep. **The die-cast police car sits on the
pillow next to his cheek**, the tiny warm-glowing light on its roof
dimmed to a whisper.

Palette: dusk blue dominant, warm cream from the lamp, terracotta on
Averill's cheek and one small glow on the car. Line tight and calm —
the closing register.

Composition: horizontal. Averill low in the frame. The window in the
top-third of the frame is where the eye rests, showing the dark trees
of the farm outside — where the giant is still sleeping.

Do not include: adults, other children (Azi is not in this frame; the
coda is Averill and the car alone), the fort, the fire engine, any
sign of the previous pages' locations. This is a bedroom. It is quiet.
