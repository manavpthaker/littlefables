# Recurring Human Character References

Use these blocks whenever Azi/Azad, Indira/Mama, Manav/Papa/Pap, or Lokijit/Loki appear in art. These are text-only likeness anchors distilled from private family reference photos. Do not commit the raw family photos to the public repo unless explicitly approved.

For image generation, paste the relevant block verbatim, then add the scene action and the shared Little Fables watercolor style anchor from `content/ART-PROMPT.md`.

## Global Human Guardrail

All family and friend characters are real human characters. Never render Azi, Indira/Mama, Manav/Papa/Pap, or Lokijit as plush, animal-like, furry, monkey-like, or muzzle-faced. Keep the plush/animal treatment only for actual plush companions such as Jujy, Dory, Pandies, Slothie, Monkie, Peter, Pooh, and Bramble.

Heritage is context, not costume. Indira/Mama is Colombian by heritage. Manav/Papa is Indian/Gujarati by heritage. They are a modern couple in their 40s. Use contemporary everyday clothing unless the page text explicitly calls for a specific cultural event or outfit. Do not put either parent in ethnic, traditional, ceremonial, folkloric, or costume clothing as shorthand for heritage.

Do not drift:

- Do not remove the red thread bracelet when the wrist is visible.

## Group Composition Rules

- Azi and Lokijit should be similar child scale; distinguish them primarily through hair silhouette: Azi has dense curls, Lokijit has straight short hair.
- Indira/Mama and Manav/Papa should feel like a modern couple in their 40s: reachable, not towering; in intimate scenes, place them physically low or leaning into Azi's space.
- Preserve recurring relationship postures: Mama down at Azi's eye level, Papa leaning close, Lokijit shoulder-to-shoulder with Azi.
- For multi-character prompts, include each character's copy prompt once, then describe the scene. Avoid rewriting the character blocks from memory.
