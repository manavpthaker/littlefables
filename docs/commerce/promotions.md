# Promotions — Sales, Offers, Codes

Drafts for every surface on Marketing → Sales and discounts. Written 2026-08-07,
depths made deliberately aggressive same day: launch conversion is the goal, margin
is explicitly second until the review base exists. Voice rules apply (warm, second
person, no exclamation marks, no emoji).

Two principles survive aggressive mode untouched:

1. **The public price never drops below the direct comp.** MagicPictureStory sells at
   $44.99. The listing price the *grid* sees stays above that line — premium relative
   position is the brand. Private, targeted codes can go deeper because a 1:1 offer
   reads as generosity, not desperation.
2. **Discount the entry, never the attach** (`add-ons.md`). Rush, PDF, and every
   future add-on listing is excluded from every sale, offer, and code, always.

## Code system

The automated offers ride on **buyer-visible promo codes** — the code text is
conversion copy. One family, one grammar: **FABLE + the moment + the number**, so the
code itself says what it's worth:

**As built 2026-08-07** (codes below are what's actually live in Shop Manager —
spellings drifted slightly from the first draft and the live versions won):

| Surface | Code | Depth | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abandoned cart | `FABLESWAIT20` | 20% | Active |
| Thank you | `NEXTFABLES15` | 15% | Active, no end date |
| Interested shopper | `FIRSTFABLE15` | 15% | **Active early — recommend pausing until Oct 1** (see §4) |
| Favorited item | `LOVEFABLES15` | 15% | Active early — harmless, leave on (see §5). Option: `LOVEFABLES20` Dec 1–15 |
| General shareable | `FABLE15` | 15% | Active through Jan 1 |
| Channel codes | `FABLEPIN15` · `FABLERAR15` · `FABLEWILD15` | 15% (20 negotiable for a big newsletter placement) | Mint as channels go live |
| Sale runs | `LAUNCH15AUG` (Aug 7–Sep 7) · `LAUNCH15SEP` (Sep 6–Oct 1, scheduled) | see §1 | Active / Scheduled |

The Run-a-sale intro price is the one surface with **no code** (automatic
strikethrough); it takes an internal name only — intent + percent + window, never
reused across runs so Details & Stats stays a readable attribution record.

## The floor math (aggressive, with eyes open)

Etsy applies codes to the current sale price, so discounts compound:

| State | Buyer pays | Net after fees | At 143 attended min |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| List | $69.00 | ~$62.00 | $26/hr |
| 20% intro sale (public) | $55.20 | ~$49.50 | $21/hr |
| Sale + `FABLEWAITS20` stacked (worst case, cart abandoners only) | $44.16 | ~$39.52 | $17/hr |
| Post-sale: list + `FIRSTFABLE15` | $58.65 | ~$52.63 | $22/hr |

The $44.16 floor sits at comp parity and is only ever seen by a cart abandoner — the
single highest-intent lost buyer there is. $17/hr on those orders is customer
acquisition cost, not a pricing strategy: each early conversion is worth more than
its margin (a sale → a review → ranking). **Reassess every depth at 25 fulfilled
books or Oct 1, whichever comes first.**

Hard limits that keep "aggressive" inside "within reason": public sale ≤25% (at 30%
the listing price dips under the $44.99 comp and the premium story breaks) · no
private code above 20% · never two automated offers at the same shopper moment.

## 6b · Friends & family (~90% off)

A straight 90% code doesn't exist — Etsy percentage codes cap at **75%**. And the
obvious alternative is closed too: **Etsy does not allow private custom listings for
digital items** (Etsy Help: "Private listings cannot be created for digital items").
Don't work around it with a physical-type private listing — wrong category, shipping
fields, and a delivery record that doesn't match the deliverable.

**The route that works — fixed-amount code.** Fixed-amount-off codes have no percent
cap (they just can't exceed item price). Marketing → Sales and discounts → Promo
code → Fixed amount off:

- Code `FABLEFAM`, no minimum, expiry a few days out.
- Amount: **$52 off while the launch sale is live** (codes stack on the sale price:
  $58.65 − $52 ≈ $6.65) · **$62 off at full price** ($69 − $62 = $7).
- Share it privately, and **deactivate it after the orders land** — a live
  fixed-amount code applies shop-wide and a leak is expensive.
- The friend orders the real listing with the code, which means the order runs the
  full pipeline — intake, previews, delivery — exactly the timed rep you need.

Guardrails either way: never buy from your own account (prohibited); log every F&F
order in `orders.csv` with per-stage minutes — these are exactly the timed
fulfillment reps `gtm-decision.md` #1 requires before ads. And keep the F&F cohort
out of the review plan: reviews come from real-price warm buyers with one honest ask
at delivery (the GTM rule), and Etsy's integrity systems flag reviews from obviously
linked accounts. F&F orders are for pipeline reps and portfolio, not social proof.

## 7 · Accept offers from buyers — OFF, permanently

Haggling on a made-to-order authored book erodes the exact premium story the listing
tells, and your attended time is fixed regardless of what the buyer pays. Aggressive
mode doesn't change this: structured codes are generosity, open haggling is a flea
market.

## 8 · Set an order minimum — SKIP for now

With one $69 listing there's no second item to reach a minimum with. Revisit only
when Rush/PDF listings exist — and probably still no, because it discounts the attach.

## 9 · Bundle items together — SKIP; the Deluxe listing is the bundle

The planned **Deluxe at $109** (book + PDF + print + MP3s, vs $105 bought separately)
is already the bundle mechanic, priced deliberately as a decision-shortcut rather
than a discount. Etsy's bundle tool would reintroduce a discount on add-ons. Pass.

## 10 · Etsy sales events — join the Q4 one when it appears

## When add-on listings launch (Rush $25, PDF $12, Deluxe $109)

Sweep every active promotion and **exclude the add-on listings from all of them** —
sale scope, all four automated offers, every code. One five-minute pass, and it's the
difference between the promotion strategy and a margin leak.
