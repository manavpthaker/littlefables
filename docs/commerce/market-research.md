# Market Research

Two research passes, August 2026. Claims below survived adversarial verification
(3 independent verifiers, 2/3 refutes kills a claim). Refuted claims are listed at the
bottom so they don't get re-cited.

## Audience

**US homeschool population: 3.4–4.3M kids, 3.4–6.3% of K-12.** Pew/NCES puts it at
3.4% (2022–23); NHERI at 6.26% (2024–25). The gap is methodology, not conflict — use
the range, never a single number. Growth accelerated sharply 2019–2021, well beyond
the historical 2–8%/yr.
Sources: [NHERI](https://nheri.org/research-facts-on-homeschooling/) ·
[Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/02/20/a-look-at-homeschooling-in-the-us/)

**The audience is far more secular than the stereotype.** Religious instruction fell
from ~2/3 of homeschool parents citing it (2012) to ~1/3 (2023), where it now ranks
*eighth* among reasons. 31% never attend religious services; 44% attend weekly. NHERI
describes the population as "atheists and Christians; conservatives, libertarians, and
liberals," 41% non-white/non-Hispanic.

**→ Implication:** position in intentional-parenting language, not
Christian-classical language. Wider net, also catches gentle-parenting and
screen-time-conscious segments who don't self-identify as homeschoolers.

**60% of US parents feel guilty about their kid's screen time.**
(Lurie Children's Hospital, June 2025, n=859. Corroborated by Lingokids at 74%,
Reviews.com at 54% — direction is unambiguous even if the exact number varies.)

**→ Implication:** a digital-only kids' product cannot be sold as "more screen time."
It must be framed as *replacement* for lower-value screen use. Every piece of copy
needs the defuser: no ads, no algorithm, no autoplay, no infinite scroll.
Source: [Lurie Children's](https://www.luriechildrens.org/en/blog/screen-time-2025/)

**Grandparents are a real secondary buyer.** 80% cite "gifts for special occasions" as
a top spending category — the single most common category in the survey. AARP puts
average gift spend at $805/yr per grandparent. A $29 book fits comfortably.
Source: [The Senior List](https://www.theseniorlist.com/research/grandparents-spending-study/)
(n=413, weighted)

**→ Implication:** build the gift-certificate flow. Grandparents may balk at PWA
installation; let them buy and hand off a printable certificate.

## Competitors

### Direct digital

**KidTeller** — the closest competitor. AI-generated illustrated personalized books
with the child's name, photo, and likeness throughout, plus parent voice-cloned
narration. Native iOS app. Freemium: $7.99/30 credits, $44.99/180 credits (~$0.25 per
generation).
Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/personalized-books-kidteller/id6759371503)

**→ Implication:** the "personalized illustrated + narrated" space is contested. Our
differentiators are craft, human QA, buyer-approved previews, PWA delivery, and the
privacy stance (they store voice clones and photos; we delete by default).

**SkazkaAI** — personalized audio stories, child as main character, name spoken
naturally throughout. Also markets illustrated books with photo covers.
Source: [skazka.ai](https://skazka.ai/us/audio-stories)

### Physical incumbents

| Brand | Price | Turnaround | Personalization | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wonderbly** | ~$29.99 soft / $35.99–45.99 hard | Not published; ~7–10 days operational | Name + a few details; story fixed | Physical |
| **Hooray Heroes** | $29.99–$46.99 | ~3–5 business days + shipping | Hair/skin/eyes presets + configurable cast | Physical |
| **I See Me** | $29.99–$34.99 | 2–4 business days + 4–9 day shipping | Name-only to character + story slots | Physical |
| **Put Me In The Story** | ~$29.99–32.99 | — | Name + photo into licensed IP | Deprecated — site 301s to Sourcebooks |

**Cross-brand Trustpilot pattern:** the dominant complaint on Wonderbly *and* Hooray
Heroes is **delivery delay around gift dates** — Christmas, Father's Day, birthdays.
"Paid for expedited, still late" recurs. Print quality complaints are second.
Personalization-depth complaints are a distant third.

**→ Implication:** delivery anxiety is the category's open wound. Lead with "no
shipping, ever."

**Corrections to earlier assumptions:**
- **Papercutz** is a kids' graphic-novel publisher, not a personalized-book brand. Ignore.
- **PersonalizedStories.com** doesn't resolve. Drop.
- Other adjacent players worth knowing: Dinkleboo, Blossom Reads ($39.99 soft /
  $59.99 hard), Imagitime ($39.99/$54.99), Magical Children's Book ($7.99 eBook),
  StoryBug, Make My Book.

### Turnaround norms

- Big brands don't publish turnaround. Wonderbly leans into slowness as craft
  ("wonderful things take more time") — but that works because the deliverable is a
  physical hardcover.
- Etsy digital-download personalized books quote **2–7 business days**. Buyers accept
  it when disclosed upfront.
- Etsy custom-illustration and pet-portrait sellers use a **preview-approval pattern
  as standard**: first proof in 24–48h, buyer approves or requests revisions, final in
  2–4 days. Our two-stage flow matches a pattern buyers already know.
- **Wonderbly's published 2025 Christmas cutoffs:** US standard Dec 17–19, UK Dec 21,
  Canada/Australia Dec 17, Ireland Dec 16.

**→ Implication:** our Dec 20 standard / Dec 22 rush cutoff is a genuine 3–5 day
advantage over every physical competitor, and it's the single most compelling reason
for a panicked late shopper to choose us.

## Channels

**Pinterest is the highest-leverage discovery channel for Christmas.**
96% of top holiday searches are unbranded. 70% of users are open to new brands for
gifting. Self-gifting wishlists build from October and peak in November; gift-for-
others searches surge in December.
Source: [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/festive-gifting-pinterest/)
— *self-published marketing content; treat as directionally true, not audited*

**→ Implication:** listing + pins live by **early October 2026** to catch the November
wishlist wave.

**Etsy economics for digital:**
- $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 payments. On $29 that's ~$3.21 (~89% net)
- Offsite Ads: pay-on-sale ~15%, non-optional above $10k/12mo
- Onsite Ads: $0.10–$1.50 CPC, avg $0.25–$0.50; gift niches $0.50–$1.00+
- Model breakeven at ~$0.60 CPC, 2–4% conversion
- **Use structured personalization fields, not "note to seller"** — free-text notes
  cause missing data
- **Review count is the moat.** New shops under 50 reviews get buried under
  5k-review incumbents regardless of quality. Plan a 60–90 day review flywheel.
