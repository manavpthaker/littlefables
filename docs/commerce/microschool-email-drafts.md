# Microschool and Co-op Email Drafts

These are first-touch drafts. Replace `[two time windows]` and `[email]` before sending. They are intentionally discovery-led: do not attach a sample, ask for child photos, or promise a free book until the recipient describes a real use case.

## What to include in every first email

Little Fables is a small, quiet story-reading experience: illustrated pages for daytime, a text-only sleepy mode at night, and narration that lets a child listen or read along. For a quick sense of the experience, share the public demo: **https://littlefables.app/sample** (“The Lantern of Round Pond”). The demo is there to show the reading experience—not to imply that a group pilot would look like that story. The proposed pilot would be built from the group’s own place, project, and language.
