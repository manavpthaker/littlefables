# Positioning

Source of truth for every piece of buyer-facing copy — Etsy listing, Typeform,
Pinterest pins, emails, gift certificate. When copy anywhere conflicts with this
file, this file wins.

## One line

The custom digital storybook you'd actually be proud to gift — your kid becomes the
main character, illustrated and narrated, opens on any iPad, delivered in days not
weeks.

## Who it's for

**Primary — intentional parents.** Parents who curate what their kids consume:
screen-time-conscious, gentle-parenting-adjacent, homeschool-adjacent. Broader than
"Christian classical homeschool" — only about a third of US homeschool parents cite
faith as a reason (down from two-thirds a decade ago), and 31% never attend religious
services. The unifying trait isn't ideology, it's the *intention* to hand-select what
enters their kid's life.

**Secondary — grandparents.** 80% cite "gifts for special occasions" as a top spending
category (~$805/yr average). A $29 personalized book fits comfortably. Needs a gift
certificate flow so the tech doesn't scare them.

## Three-line pitch

> Your kid, in their own storybook.
> Written for who they are, illustrated in a style you helped choose, narrated with care.
> Delivered in days — and saved to their iPad like a favorite app.

## Differentiators, in priority order

### 1. Faster than anyone else with real personalization
Style previews in 24 hours. Final book in 3–4 days. Because we're digital, there are no
shipping windows — ordered Dec 20? Delivered before Christmas. Wonderbly quotes 7–10
days and cuts off Dec 17–19. **We accept orders through Dec 22 with rush.**

This is the loudest single marketing beat. Every competitor's top complaint on
Trustpilot is delivery anxiety. We turn their weakness into our headline.

### 2. Curated, not AI slop
Every book gets a human QA pass. Every art style is generated from *your* book
inspirations, not picked from a canned dropdown of five. Every character rendered to
match *your* kid, not swapped from a template. This is what justifies our price above
KidTeller's $7.99 tier: taste, care, one book done right.

### 3. Privacy by default
We delete the intake once the book is delivered — unless the buyer actively opts in to
keep their kid's profile for future books. No voice clones stored. No accounts
required. No data used to train anything. This defuses the AI-freakout that KidTeller
triggers ("clone your voice, save your kid's photo").

### 4. Screen-time that earns its place
No ads. No algorithm. No autoplay. No infinite scroll. No "next book suggested." A
quiet 20-minute moment with your kid inside their own story. Defuses the biggest
positioning wall: 60% of US parents feel guilty about their kid's screen time (Lurie
Children's, 2025, n=859).

### 5. Grandparent-friendly
Gift certificate PDF that grandma can print or email; recipient parent redeems in one
link. No PWA-install-scaring-grandma required to purchase.

## Trust guarantee

> **Unlimited revisions on your previews until you love them. If we still haven't hit
> it, full refund.**

Unbounded promise, bounded reality: soft-cap at 3 rounds operationally. Most buyers
land in 1–2. If someone hits round 4+, quietly convert to refund. The listing says
"unlimited or your money back," which is the strongest possible trust artifact.

## Voice

Warm. Second person. Present tense. Never corporate.

**Never use:** "AI-powered," "revolutionary," "next-gen," "cutting-edge," exclamation
marks in body copy, emoji in buyer-facing copy, corporate voice.

## What we are NOT

- Not a story-generator platform (one curated experience, not a maker app)
- Not a subscription (per-book pricing)
- Not a chatbot (warm human-designed intake)
- Not freemium (every book is a paid gift-quality artifact)
- Not physical (digital is the *feature*, not a compromise)
