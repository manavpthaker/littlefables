```
kid? If the answer is no, it doesn't go out. That's why you approve the
character before I build anything, why revisions are unlimited, and why you
get every dollar back if I can't get it right.

Two things I care about, since you're deciding whether to trust me with your
child's name.

I delete your questionnaire once your book is delivered. Not saved, not
stored, not used to train anything — unless you tell me to keep it so your
next book is one click away.

And there's nothing in the book trying to get your kid to do anything next.
No ads, no autoplay, no algorithm, no suggested title when it ends. Twenty
quiet minutes, and then it's over.

If you have a question before you order, message me. It's me answering.
```

## 3 · Tagline

**Limit is 55 characters** — verified in the field, not guessed.

Current: `Stories as unique as your little one.` (37)

It's pleasant but it's a claim any personalized-book shop could make, and it
doesn't say what the thing *is*. The tagline sits directly under your shop
name in search results and on the shop page — it's worth spending on the
differentiator instead.

| Chars | Option | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | `Your kid, in their own storybook.` | **Recommended.** Your own positioning one-liner. Says exactly what it is, no adjectives, and "in their own" quietly does the your-kid-*is*-the-character work. |
| 48 | `Your kid, in their own storybook. Ready in days.` | Same, plus the speed beat — your loudest differentiator, and the thing every competitor's reviews complain about. |
| 50 | `Your kid is the main character. No shipping, ever.` | Leads on delivery anxiety. Strongest in December, weaker the rest of the year. |
| 41 | `One kid, one book, written just for them.` | Craft-forward. Pairs with the About headline. |

## Two things to fix while you're in there

**1. The About section is currently your listing copy.** The headline is the
full 129-character listing title and the body is the listing description
pasted in — including the `━━━` separators, which the About field collapsed
into run-on text (`...delivered in 3–4 days. ━━━ HOW IT WORKS 1 · You tell us
about your kid A short, warm questionnaire...`).

That's worth replacing for three reasons: a buyer who clicks About has already
read the listing, so it gives them nothing new; the broken separators are the
first formatting a skeptical buyer sees from a shop with zero reviews; and
About is the one place on Etsy where a person gets to talk, which is the only
advantage you have over Wonderbly.

**2. "I" here vs "we" in the listing.** The listing copy is all *we*; this is
all *I*. That's normal on Etsy — marketing voice on the listing, personal
voice on the About page — and I'd leave it. If it bothers you, the single line
worth aligning is the listing's AI FAQ: "we're not interested in shipping
something we wouldn't give our own kid" → "I'm not interested in shipping
something I wouldn't give my own kid." That one lands harder in first person
anyway, since it's the same promise this bio is built on.
