# Audit of the Final-Model Report

Two independent passes, August 17 2026: a web fact-check of the ten decision-critical claims,
and an internal consistency/arithmetic/prompt-compliance audit against the prompt and the three
prior reports. **Verdict: the model's shape survives; its price and several gates do not.**

## Bottom line after audit

**Keep:** semi-custom human-written stylized new-sibling/milestone hardcover, physical base +
digital companion add-on, no narrated app, honest "Designed by + AI-assisted art, human-written
story" Etsy positioning, stylized-only (no face-geometry likeness), photos deleted, proof
approval, revision cap.

**Change the price.** Two independent audit findings point the same direction:

1. **The $109 math was wrong — in $109's favor.** Correctly computed, $109 nets **$63.19/order
   (~$25/hr at 2.5 hrs)** vs $89's $35.75–45.09 (~$14.30–18.04/hr). $89 never clears the $18/hr
   floor in any honestly-computed year-1 scenario; $109 clears it comfortably.
2. **The factual pillar under "premium fails on demand" collapsed.** RNBishere — cited as proof
   the only $145+ solo survivor is off-category — **currently sells in-category personalized
   children's story books at ~$155–158 sale / ~$316 list, 5.0★** (live shop check, Aug 17 2026:
   607 sales, 290 reviews, incl. a July 2026 children's-book review). An in-category solo
   premium seller exists. (Caveat: its 607 sales include $33 digital book-cover downloads, so
   premium-book velocity is unknowable from outside.)

**Corrected call: launch at $109 base (list ~$149), run the price split test as $89 vs $109
instead of assuming $89, and let the test — not the report — settle it.** The $30–60
new-sibling spend norm remains the open risk at any premium price; the occasion split test
(new-sibling vs first-birthday vs adoption — restore the adoption arm) is what resolves it.

## Internal audit — defects that change decisions

**Fatal (all three cut against $89, none against the shape):**

1. **Offsite Ads breaks the only floor-clearing scenario.** The "steady state, offsite opted
   out" case is impossible at the plan's own volumes — trailing sales cross $10k right as
   steady state begins, making 12% participation mandatory for shop life. Re-applying the
   report's own blended-attribution logic: steady-state net ≈ $42 → **$16.97/hr, below the $18
   floor**. At $109 the same correction still clears ~$23–25/hr.
2. **The recommendation trips its own kill rule at launch.** Launch economics run
   $14.30–17.88/hr; the report's hard stop is "<$15/hr by order #5" — and the first five orders
   are by definition launch-economics orders.
3. **The month-9 kill criterion ("<15 orders/mo → fix or stop") fires on the plan's own
   month-9–10 target of 9–12 orders.** Repair: kill criteria must be consistent with the plan
   trajectory (e.g., "<8 orders/mo by month 9").

**Material:**

- **$109 arithmetic:** costs sum to $45.81, net **$63.19**, ~$25/hr — the report stated $56–58
  and ~$22/hr, understating the strongest alternative to its chosen price.
- **December is over capacity:** 20–25 "capped" orders = 40–62.5 hrs against a ~43-hr month;
  the honest cap is **~17 orders**. Express-shipping margin erosion (~$9/order) was dropped
  exactly where it bites.
- **The cutoff math yields ~Nov 28–Dec 1**, not Dec 3, before adding queue depth. Use **Dec 1**.
- **Dropped red-team gates — restored:** do not proceed if 30 days + $100 ads yields <3 orders;
  stop if two consecutive months net under $300 after 25+ reviews; drop digital add-on if it
  drives >50% of refunds/complaints.
- **Deletion promise still not settled by self-hosted links:** the print-ready PDF (child's
  name + likeness art) goes to Lulu, which retains sold-project files by default. The promise
  ships only with a written per-order deletion confirmation or DPA — or it gets reworded.
- **Unruled items:** the special-situations digital SKU was deleted without a ruling (the
  adoption/foster "generic books failed us" demand is real; park it as a later content-strategy
  SKU at ~$49, not a launch product). Turnaround promise never set despite "arrives on time"
  ranking #1 — set one (e.g., proof in 5 days, delivered in ~3 weeks standard). Sales
  tax (marketplace-collected on Etsy), entity/insurance, and FTC AI-disclosure basics were
  requested and never delivered — close these before launch, none are blockers.

**Minor:** add-on incremental cost is ~$2.38 not $1.60 (net ~$22.60 stands); "same as her
cousin's" was the prompt's own illustrative phrase, not found review language; review-rate
assumptions in the 12-month table (70–90% of orders leaving reviews) are optimistic — typical
is 20–40%, so review milestones land later than charted.

## Corrected spec (what actually survives all five documents)

- **Product:** unchanged from the final report (semi-custom situation-story stylized hardcover,
  human-written, proof-approved, +$25–29 digital companion, no app).
- **Price:** **$109 sale / ~$149 list** base case; split-test $89 vs $109 in weeks 3–4 instead
  of presuming either; RNBishere's live prices (~$155 in-category) license testing upward, not
  just down.
- **Floor:** $18/hr stands, but honest year-1 hourly at $89 is below it; at $109 it clears
  with margin for the mandatory Offsite Ads 12%.
- **Occasion:** new-sibling first, but the $30–60 occasion norm vs premium price is unresolved —
  the three-arm occasion test (sibling / first birthday / adoption) is the decider, and
  "milestone keepsake" framing is the fallback if sibling caps out low.
- **Calendar:** Dec 1 new-order cutoff; ~17-order December cap; gift certificates after.
- **Kill criteria (repaired):** no-go if <3 orders from 30 days + $100 ads; stop if <$15/hr by
  order #5 or two consecutive months <$300 net after 25+ reviews; <8 orders/mo by month 9 →
  fix listing/quality before any price move; review rating <4.8 without 60-day recovery → pause.
- **Honest expectation:** ~$150–300/mo net most of year 1; $500/mo is a month-10-plus outcome
  at ~8–11 orders/mo at $109 economics; $1,000 is a December spike at the capacity cap.
