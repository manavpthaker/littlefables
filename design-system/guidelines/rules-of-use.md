# Rules of use

**The test**: if it would look at home on a 2023 Etsy shop, it's wrong. If it would look at home in a bookshop that's been selling children's classics since 1928, it's right.

## Always
- One oxblood primary action per screen; focus ring 3px navy at 2px offset.
- Reading text over art sits on --wash-panel (or inside a scrim as --text-on-art). Only scrim / capsule / panel / sheet may sit over art.
- Press settles (--shadow-press) — elements never vanish under the finger.
- Ornaments are aria-hidden decoration. One fleuron placed with care beats twenty scattered; filigree only on ceremonial surfaces.
- Kid errors are in-world characters; mercy is brass at 40%, never red.
- Names are data; kid copy is spoken-first, ≤ 12 words per UI utterance; numerals written out.
- Every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion equivalent (tick→instant, wind/settle→fade, chime→brightness, idle→static).
- Contrast: kid body ≥ 4.5:1, parent body ≥ 8:1, on every lighting stage.

## Never
- White backgrounds (ColoringPage excepted), gray neutrals, sans-serif, handwriting/script fonts, emoji.
- Colored/tinted shadows, glow, neumorphism; --shadow-float combined with an ornamental border.
- Material patterns: chips, FABs, snackbars. Cartoon mascots. Skeuomorphic textures.
- Dark surfaces outside [data-bedtime] (kid-only; Parent Corner and outward never render dark).
- Animation on outward surfaces — the mark may breathe on splash only.
- --gilt on body text; --ink-faint carrying meaning; --danger on kid surfaces.
